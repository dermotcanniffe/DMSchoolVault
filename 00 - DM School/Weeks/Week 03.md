## 📝 Notes from class
> What did you learn? Jot it down in your own words.

- An NPC really only needs a **description** and **one point of view** to be playable.
- A point of view is "what does a person think, and why" — often a bond, ideal, or flaw.
- Quick way in: does the NPC like/dislike their situation? Like/dislike the PCs (strangers)?

## 💡 Key takeaways
1. Don't be precious with the POV — it's a jumping-off point the scene can shift.
2. Give the NPC a clear objective (what they want).
3. Define one **trigger** that flips them friendly ↔ unfriendly (trust made, deepened, or broken).
