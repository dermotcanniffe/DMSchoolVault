## The dice, in plain words
"d" just means "die". The number after it is how many sides it has — so the biggest number you can roll.

| Die | Biggest number | Used for |
|-----|----------------|----------|
| d4  | 4  | small damage (a dagger) |
| d6  | 6  | common damage, many things |
| d8  | 8  | weapon damage |
| d10 | 10 | bigger damage, percent tens |
| d12 | 12 | biggest single weapon (greataxe) |
| **d20** | **20** | **almost every action & check** |
| d100 | 100 | random tables (roll two d10s) |

**"2d6" means:** roll two 6-sided dice and add them together. The first number = how many dice. You never have to multiply — just roll that many and total them.

## How a roll works (the only pattern you need)
> Roll a d20. Add a bonus. Compare to a target number. Equal or higher = success.

**Roll + bonus ≥ target → you succeed.**

That's it. Every check, attack, and save is this one sentence.
- The **bonus** is written on the character/monster sheet. You don't calculate it — you read it.
- The **target** is either a DC (see [[02 - Reference/Difficulty and DCs|Difficulty & DCs]]) or an enemy's AC (see [[02 - Reference/Combat and Encounter Math|Combat & Encounter Math]]).

## Adding the bonus without stress
You only ever add **one d20 result + one small bonus**. If adding in your head is hard:
- Count up from the bigger number. (17, then +3 → 18, 19, 20.)
- Or let a dice app total it. **Obsidian tip:** the *Dice Roller* community plugin rolls and adds for you inline.
- Or just say the d20 number out loud and let a player add their own bonus. Totally normal.

## Advantage & disadvantage (no math at all)
- **Advantage:** roll **two** d20s, keep the **higher**. (Something's in your favor.)
- **Disadvantage:** roll **two** d20s, keep the **lower**. (Something's working against you.)
- They don't stack and they cancel each other out. If you have both, you roll one die normally.

## Natural 20 / natural 1
- **Natural 20** = the d20 itself shows 20. On an attack, that's a *critical hit* (extra damage — see combat sheet). Always feels great; let it.
- **Natural 1** on an attack always misses. (For skill checks, a 1 is just a low number, not an automatic fail — that's a house rule if you want it.)
