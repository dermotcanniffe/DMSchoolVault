# 🎯 Difficulty & DCs

⬅ [[02 - Reference/Reference Home|Reference Home]]

**DC = Difficulty Class.** It's just the target number a player needs to hit (or beat) with their roll. Higher DC = harder.

## The only table you need — pick a word, use the number

| How hard is it? | DC | Feels like |
|-----------------|----|-----------|
| Very easy       | 5  | almost anyone does it |
| Easy            | 10 | a competent person usually does it |
| Medium          | 15 | trained people succeed, others struggle |
| Hard            | 20 | experts and lucky rolls |
| Very hard       | 25 | heroic, rare |
| Nearly impossible | 30 | legendary |

**How to use it:** decide the vibe ("that lock looks *hard*"), read across to the DC (20), tell the player "give me a DC 20 check." Done. No math.

> [!tip] When in doubt, use 15
> DC 15 ("medium") is the safe default for almost anything. If you can't decide, say 15 and move on.

## Should you even ask for a roll?
- **No roll needed** if the task is trivial *or* impossible, or if failure is boring. Just say what happens.
- **Roll** only when success and failure are both interesting.

## Which ability fits the task?
Not sure what to call for? Match the action to a word:

| The player is trying to... | Ask for a... |
|----------------------------|--------------|
| smash, lift, grapple | Strength |
| dodge, sneak, balance, aim | Dexterity |
| endure, resist poison, hold breath | Constitution |
| recall lore, investigate, solve | Intelligence |
| notice, sense motive, survive outdoors | Wisdom |
| persuade, deceive, intimidate, perform | Charisma |

You don't need the exact skill name. "Make a Wisdom check to notice something" is completely fine.

## Passive checks (zero rolling)
Sometimes you don't want to roll — you just want to know if a character notices something. Use their **passive score**, written on their sheet (often "Passive Perception"). If the DC of the hidden thing is *at or below* that number, they notice it automatically. No dice, no math — compare two written numbers.
