# **Build a PC Backstory**

Make a copy of this to fill out  
Use proper nouns\!

1. your character's name, species, gender, age  
2. Who their nemesis is (someone who did, is currently, or will try to thwart their goals)  
3. Greatest strength  
4. Greatest flaw  
5. What/who they want most  
6. What/who they fear most  
7. Family\! Or chosen family. Include at least one name of someone within a work environment and one name outside a work environment.  
8. Some sort of back story--doesn't need to be long, maybe three sentences (but you could also write a full page or several\!--whatever you want). Feel free to make up names and town names and events--there's room for everyone to be a storyteller\! If it stresses you out, though, don't worry about it--I gotchoo. This should simply be fun, and if it's not, don't do it\!  
9. A descriptor for your character. Something like a log line for your character. (Ex: The anxious, socially awkward genius wizard. or The wide-eyed idealistic hippy druid.)  
10. The following, I’m adding just for this exercise:  
    1. What adventure did you just complete and what were some complications that got in your way?   
       1. Geography, what was the quest, any monsters defeated, reward, failures/deaths, other complications?  
    2. Where do you stand now with specifically two of your adventuring companions?  
11. Don’t answer this, but I usually ask something like this to my players or we build this together in person–this was for a table of 3 players: One way you made EACH OF YOUR PARTY MEMBERS' LIVES EASIER in the last five years (so you'll write two), and one way you made EACH OF YOUR PARTY MEMBERS' LIVES WORSE in the last five years (again, you'll write two)
