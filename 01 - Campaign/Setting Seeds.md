# 🌱 Setting Seeds

⬅ [[01 - Campaign/Campaign Home|Campaign Home]]

One-line location ideas for one-shots and exercises. Pick one and expand it with the [[03 - Templates/Setting Seed|Setting Seed template]] or the [[02 - Reference/Using AI Helpers|AI helpers]].

## Favourites (fleshed a little)
1. The Sword Coast Office of Magical Conveyances — essentially a Medieval/Magical DMV.
2. A wildcat mine, run by halflings who know very little about mining, trying to keep it secret from the nearby dwarves.
3. A diner with a wildfire outside and an immediate lockdown order — and roughly half the customers are vampires.
4. A marketplace on a floating town on the icy surface of Europa.
