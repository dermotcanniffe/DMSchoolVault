# Location: Oakhaven (The City of Bridges & Books)

⬅ [[01 - Campaign/Campaign Home|Campaign Home]]

- **Location:** Built across a massive river canyon in a temperate forest region.
- **Governance:** Ruled by an elected **High Council of Guildmasters** representing trade, arcana, civic safety, and public works.
- **Atmosphere:** Bustling, scholarly, and multicultural. Gondolas and stone bridges connect towering multi-level districts carved into the canyon walls.

## Key City Districts
- **The Stacks (Library District):** A labyrinthine quarter built around a massive central archive. It houses everything from public reading halls to magical vaults.
- **The Low Docks:** The river-level market district filled with barges, fishmongers, tavern grifters, and warehouse syndicates.
- **The Spire Ward:** High on the canyon rim, home to noble estates, wizarding academies, and the High Council hall.

## Current hook
> **PC:** You're investigating the increase in magical deaths in the city, with a letter of mark from the local Elector. You're new to the city and proceeding more blind than usual. So you go to someone who would have some city knowledge — **Sara Highwater**.
