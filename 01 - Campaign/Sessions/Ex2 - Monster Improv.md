# Ex 2, Option 2 — Monster Improv

> A talking monster who knows the PC is here to slay them, and doesn't want to die. Aim: a scene where trust can be built, deepened, or broken.
> Handout: [[00 - DM School/Weeks/Week 03 - NPC Primer (curriculum)|Week 3 curriculum]] · Guided prompt: [[gemini-scribe/Prompts/ex2-monster-improv|Monster Improv prompt]]

## The build
- **PC objective (why slay it):**  Bimble Poon, the Campestri, that the villagers think is a Myconid.
- **Creature & gender:** 
- **Point of view & why:** Carefree with the joys of spring
- **What the monster wants:** to sing, and spore! 
- **Feelings toward the PC & why:** happiness and generosity
- **Trust trigger (and what happens if it fires):** implicit trust of all things, but doesn't want to die
- **Moment before (what it's doing + emotional state):** singing, happy
- **Relation to PC (optional):** 
- **Three things it knows the PC doesn't** (★ = ties to objective / reason not to kill):
  1. Can call upon it's fellow campestris to defend the village
  2. The village needs defending because there is a great threat looming
  3. ★ knows what the threat is! And how to defeat it!
- **Location / fantasy geography:** The woods, near a village

## Notes

> [!example]- 📊 Stat Block — Phycomid (the *real* threat the villagers fear)
> *Medium Plant, Unaligned*
>
> - **Armor Class:** 11 (natural armor)
> - **Hit Points:** 39 (6d8 + 12)
> - **Speed:** 15 ft.
>
> | STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
> |:-:|:-:|:-:|:-:|:-:|:-:|
> | 12 (+1) | 8 (−1) | 14 (+2) | 1 (−5) | 10 (+0) | 1 (−5) |
>
> - **Condition Immunities:** Blinded, deafened, frightened, prone
> - **Senses:** Tremorsense 30 ft., passive Perception 10
> - **Languages:** —
> - **Challenge:** 2 (450 XP) · **Proficiency Bonus:** +2
>
> **False Appearance.** While the phycomid remains motionless, it is indistinguishable from a mundane, non-monstrous patch of fungi and decaying detritus.
>
> **Fungal Rebirth.** If a Medium or smaller beast or humanoid dies while infected by the phycomid's *Spore Infestation*, 1d4 new tiny phycomids sprout from the corpse 1 minute later. These become fully grown Medium phycomids after 24 hours.
>
> **Actions**
> - **Multiattack.** The phycomid makes two attacks: one with its Fibrous Tendril and one with its Caustic Globule.
> - **Fibrous Tendril.** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +3 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 4 (1d6 + 1) bludgeoning damage plus 4 (1d7) acid damage.
> - **Caustic Globule.** *Ranged Weapon Attack:* +1 to hit, range 20/60 ft., one target. *Hit:* 7 (2d6) acid damage. If the target is a creature, it must succeed on a **DC 12 Constitution saving throw** or become infected with *Spore Infestation*.
>
> **The Spore Infestation (Disease)**
> While infected, a creature takes 7 (2d6) necrotic damage at the start of each of its turns as fungal filaments consume its tissue. The infected creature can repeat the saving throw at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on a success. The infection also ends immediately if the creature receives any effect that cures diseases or neutralizes poisons.
