Here are the revised stat blocks scaled specifically for a Level 3 solo encounter. The goblins have been reduced to CR 1/4 enemies so they do not instantly overwhelm Alvyn's 17 Hit Points, and the allied NPCs have been converted into CR 1/8 support characters with low damage and utility-focused abilities so Alvyn remains the primary damage dealer and hero.

**Grolk (Goblin Guard)**
_Small humanoid (goblinoid), Neutral_
- **Armor Class:** 13 (Rusty Chain Shirt fragments)
- **Hit Points:** 14 (4d6) 
- **Speed:** 30 ft.
- **STR** 13 (+1) | **DEX** 10 (+0) | **CON** 11 (+0) | **INT** 7 (-2) | **WIS** 8 (-1) | **CHA** 8 (-1)
- **Senses:** Darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 9
- **Dull Reflexes:** Grolk has disadvantage on Dexterity saving throws.
- **Actions:** _Morningstar._ _Melee Weapon Attack:_ +3 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. _Hit:_ 5 (1d8 + 1) piercing damage. 
- Draught of Uncontainable Might

**Blag (Goblin Guard)**
_Small humanoid (goblinoid), Neutral_
- **Armor Class:** 14 (Thick Hides, Improvised Door-Shield)  1
- **Hit Points:** 11 (3d6 + 0)
- **Speed:** 25 ft.
- **STR** 12 (+1) | **DEX** 9 (-1) | **CON** 11 (+0) | **INT** 6 (-2) | **WIS** 8 (-1) | **CHA** 9 (-1)
- **Senses:** Darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 9
- **Iron Bucket Helmet:** Blag has advantage on saving throws against being blinded, but disadvantage on all Wisdom (Perception) checks.
- **Actions:** _Door Bash._ _Melee Weapon Attack:_ +3 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. _Hit:_ 3 (1d4 + 1) bludgeoning damage.

**Thora Ironspoke (The Ticking Clock)**
_Medium humanoid (dwarf), Chaotic Neutral_
- **Armor Class:** 11 (Unarmored)
- **Hit Points:** 13 (2d8 + 4)
- **Speed:** 25 ft.
- **STR** 10 (+0) | **DEX** 12 (+1) | **CON** 14 (+2) | **INT** 16 (+3) | **WIS** 10 (+0) | **CHA** 9 (-1)
- **Senses:** Darkvision 60 ft.  
- **Actions:** _Frantic Tinkering._ Thora works on a mechanical lock, trap, or door. She requires three consecutive actions using this trait to open the cell block's main exit. _Heavy Wrench._ _Melee Weapon Attack:_ +2 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. _Hit:_ 2 (1d4) bludgeoning damage.

**Kaelen "The Anvil" Vanii (The Meat Shield)**
_Medium humanoid (goliath), Lawful Neutral_
- **Armor Class:** 12 (Thick Skin)
- **Hit Points:** 22 (3d8 + 9)
- **Speed:** 30 ft.
- **STR** 16 (+3) | **DEX** 10 (+0) | **CON** 16 (+3) | **INT** 10 (+0) | **WIS** 12 (+1) | **CHA** 9 (-1)
- **Living Barricade:** Any ally standing directly behind Kaelen gains half cover (+2 to AC and Dexterity saving throws) from attacks originating in front of him.
- **Actions:** _Unarmed Strike._ _Melee Weapon Attack:_ +5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. _Hit:_ 4 (1d2 + 3) bludgeoning damage.





**Pip (The Secret Weapon)**
_Small humanoid (halfling), Neutral Good_
- **Armor Class:** 13 (Unarmored)
- **Hit Points:** 10 (3d6)
- **Speed:** 30 ft.
- **STR** 8 (-1) | **DEX** 16 (+3) | **CON** 10 (+0) | **INT** 11 (+0) | **WIS** 14 (+2) | **CHA** 13 (+1)
- **Reactions:** _Clumsy Intercept._ If an ally within 5 feet of Pip is hit by an attack, Pip can use his reaction to "accidentally" trip into the attacker, imposing disadvantage on the attack roll retroactively, potentially turning the hit into a miss.
- **Actions:** _Frying Pan._ _Melee Weapon Attack:_ +5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. _Hit:_ 5 (1d4 + 3) bludgeoning damage.

**Silas Thorne (The Tactician)**
_Medium humanoid (half-elf), Chaotic Neutral_
- **Armor Class:** 12 (Unarmored)
- **Hit Points:** 11 (2d8 + 2)
- **Speed:** 30 ft.
- **STR** 9 (-1) | **DEX** 14 (+2) | **CON** 12 (+1) | **INT** 14 (+2) | **WIS** 10 (+0) | **CHA** 16 (+3)
- **Senses:** Darkvision 60 ft.
- **Bonus Actions:** _Sideline Coaching._ Silas shouts out an enemy's blind spot. One ally of Silas's choice gains advantage on their next attack roll before the start of Silas's next turn.
- **Actions:** _Concealed Shiv._ _Melee Weapon Attack:_ +4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. _Hit:_ 4 (1d4 + 2) piercing damage.

**Lyra Vance (The Support)**
_Medium humanoid (tiefling), Chaotic Neutral_
- **Armor Class:** 11 (Unarmored)
- **Hit Points:** 13 (2d8 + 4)
- **Speed:** 30 ft.
- **STR** 8 (-1) | **DEX** 12 (+1) | **CON** 14 (+2) | **INT** 12 (+1) | **WIS** 12 (+1) | **CHA** 16 (+3)
- **Senses:** Darkvision 60 ft.
- **Bonus Actions:** _Humming Inspiration (2/Day)._ Lyra grants a 1d6 Inspiration die to one ally within 30 feet who can hear her humming.
- **Actions:** _Vicious Mockery (Cantrip)._ Lyra targets one creature she can see within 60 feet. It must succeed on a DC 13 Wisdom saving throw or take 2 (1d4) psychic damage and have disadvantage on its next attack roll before the end of its next turn.
